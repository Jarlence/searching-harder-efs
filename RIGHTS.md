# Rights and attribution

The project software has no assigned project-wide open-source license. Reuse is subject to the applicable rights and licenses.

Third-party datasets retain their original licenses and attribution requirements. The eight additional UCI datasets are distributed under their source records' CC BY 4.0 terms, including the applicable conditions for processed derivatives. [DATA_SOURCES.md](DATA_SOURCES.md) provides the creators, source pages, dataset DOIs, license link, and study-specific transformations.

The R22 collection supplies source descriptions and acquisition scripts for the original datasets. Data retrieved with those scripts remain subject to their source terms. Software dependencies retain their own licenses.
